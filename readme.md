# Greeting Card Generator
Made this Greeting card generator why u may ask because I have many friends and family members to wish on different different occasions be it birthday, Relatives son/daughter marriage or neighbours marriage, festivals(my country has a LOT of festivals like Diwali, Eid, Chath Puja, Holi, Bakrid and many more) and more at that situtation if we can greet them with a card that too a digital one will be such a good thing.

## About This Greeting Card Generator
On visiting this website you will be welcomed on the Home page of Greeting cards from whre you will be taken to the custom Greeting card maker by clicking the click t move to cards page,
There you will have a simple home page button on the nav bar and two options to choose from according to what you want exactly,
if you want to just greet with texts and emojis with white background and nothing else then click the Greeter (with texts and emojis only) and if you want more fredom and also want to put your own desired background image than you can choose from your internal files and generate one.
